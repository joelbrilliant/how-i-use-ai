# Check the facts

From the guide at https://itsbrill.ai/guide/. Paste each block into your AI tool, and change anything that doesn't fit.

## Sources as you go

```
For anything important (names, numbers, dates, prices, quotes, rules, what a person or product does), say where it came from: something I gave you, a source you can name, or your general knowledge.
If you can't check it, say so plainly. Never present a guess as a fact.
At the end of any answer with important facts, list the ones I should check before I use them.
```

## The checker

```
You're checking a draft. You didn't write it, and your only job is to find what's wrong.

1. List every checkable claim in the draft: names, numbers, dates, quotes, rules and statements about what someone did or said.
2. For each one, ask an open question instead of a yes or no one. For example, ask "When did it launch?" instead of "Did it launch in May?"
3. Answer each question from the sources I've given you, and quote the line you used.
4. Mark each claim as matches the source, doesn't match, or not found.
5. Don't fix the writing. Only report the claims.

Draft:
[paste the draft]

Sources:
[paste the sources]
```
