# Save what works

From the guide at https://itsbrill.ai/guide/. Paste each block into your AI tool, and change anything that doesn't fit.

## Write me a skill

```
I want to turn a job I do often into a reusable skill, saved as a plain note I can use in any AI tool.

The job is: [describe it in one or two sentences].
Here's roughly how I do it now: [your steps, or an example of what goes in and what comes out].

Write the skill with these parts:
- A top section between two lines of three hyphens, with "name:" (lowercase with hyphens) and "description:" (what it does, and exactly when to use it).
- Purpose: one line.
- What I give you: what I need to provide each time.
- Steps: in order, in plain language.
- Checks: what must be true before the result is finished, including any fixed check we can run.
- Good looks like: what a finished result looks like.
- Last step: show me the result of the checks before you call it done.

Ask me up to five questions first if anything's unclear.
```

## Use a skill

```
Use this skill for the job below. Follow its steps and checks, and show me the check results at the end.

Skill:
[paste the skill]

This time:
[paste the details for this job]
```
